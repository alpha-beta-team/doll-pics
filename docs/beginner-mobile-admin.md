# Doll Pictures mobile admin — one-page training guide

**Main rule: Every new customer goes into Enquiries first.**

1. Open **Today** every morning and work from top to bottom.
2. Tap the blue **+** button to record a caller, WhatsApp lead, walk-in, referral, or active diary lead. Name and phone are enough.
3. Use **Call** or **WhatsApp**, then save the next follow-up. Tap **Done** after speaking or **Tomorrow** when the customer asks for more time.
4. When the customer confirms, open the enquiry, tap **Convert**, choose the shoot date, and confirm the booking. Do not create a second booking.
5. When money arrives, open **Payments**, tap **Record**, enter the amount and method, and save.

## First-week checklist

- Enter only upcoming shoots, unpaid balances, and pending follow-ups from the diary.
- Keep the diary and website together for seven days.
- The owner checks both every evening.
- After one clean week, the website becomes the main record.
- Never enable automatic WhatsApp messages unless the customer clearly agrees.

The same illustrated guide is available inside the admin under **More → Quick Guide**.
